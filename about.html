Since 2014

Started with one wheel in a garage.

Ember & Clay began when our founder, Maren, started throwing mugs on a secondhand wheel to give away at her sister's coffee shop. Twelve years later we're eight people, three kilns, and one very old sewing machine — still making things one at a time.

How we got here

A short, slow history

2014

The garage wheel

Maren throws her first hundred mugs for a coffee shop three doors down. They sell out in a weekend.

2017

The first studio

We move into a converted barn outside Brattleboro and bring on our first two potters.

2021

Textiles join the bench

A stitching corner opens up — aprons and baskets start appearing alongside the ceramics.

2026

Eight makers, one kiln room

Still small on purpose. Every order still ships from the same barn.
